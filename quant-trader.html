Quant Trader (Crypto)
We’re hiring a quant to research, build, and trade systematic crypto strategies. You will own the full loop: data → hypothesis → model → execution → monitoring. We care about rigor, clean engineering, and reality-checking everything against market microstructure.

>the mission

Build robust systematic strategies for crypto markets, and deploy them into production with disciplined risk controls and operational reliability. This role is for someone who can move fast, but never ships fantasy.

role: "quant trader" asset_class: "crypto" focus: "systematic research + execution" bar: "high rigor, low ego"
>what you will do
1.
Research alpha hypotheses (trend, mean-reversion, funding, basis, microstructure, regime models).
2.
Build clean backtests and realistic simulations (fees, slippage, latency, partial fills, funding, liquidations).
3.
Design execution logic (order placement, throttling, adaptive sizing, spread/impact awareness).
4.
Own risk systems: limits, drawdown controls, kill-switches, exposure caps, stress tests.
5.
Monitor live performance and improve production reliability (alerts, metrics, post-trade analysis).
>what we value

You are comfortable being wrong fast and correcting faster. You love clean data and clean code. You can explain decisions and assumptions. You don’t chase shiny metrics without understanding the underlying mechanics.

>requirements
-
Strong statistics / probability intuition and the ability to formalize hypotheses.
-
Solid Python for research and tooling (pandas, numpy, vectorized workflows).
-
Experience with market microstructure and execution constraints.
-
Ability to build/validate backtests that avoid common traps (leakage, survivorship, overfitting).
-
Comfortable with production thinking: monitoring, failure modes, safety rails.
stack: Python, PostgreSQL, Linux venues: major centralized exchanges execution: API-based
